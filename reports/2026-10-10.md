## NVDA

### What were the most recent quarterly financial results?

For the second quarter ended July 26, 2026 (Q2 FY27), NVIDIA reported revenue of **$96.2 billion**, up 18% from the prior quarter and 106% year‑over‑year.  
Gross margin was **75.0 %**.  
Operating income was **$63.7 billion** and net income **$59.7 billion**, resulting in diluted earnings per share of **$2.46**.
